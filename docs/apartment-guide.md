## Heating

There are two thermostats in the apartment. One next to the TV in the living
room, one in the bathroom.

- **Set temperature**: Use knob (1) to adjust the temperature.
- **Heating indicator**: (3) lights orange when actively heating, white when the
  set temperature is reached and maintained.
- **Heating on/off indicators**: (4) white when heating is on, (5) white when
  heating is off. Use the switch (2) to turn heating on/off.
