# House Rules

To make sure everyone has a pleasant and comfortable stay, please follow our
rules:

## 🏠 General

- Please treat the home with care, as if it were your own.
- Only registered guests are allowed to stay overnight.
- Quiet hours are from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM.
- Please be mindful of neighbors and keep noise to a minimum during this time.

## 🎉 No Parties or Events

- Parties, events, or large gatherings are not allowed.
- Please keep noise at a respectful level at all times.

## 🚭 No Smoking

- Smoking is strictly prohibited inside the apartment.
- This includes cigarettes, cigars, vapes, and e-cigarettes.
- Smoking is allowed outside, please dispose of cigarette butts properly.

## 🐾 Pets

- Small pets only, and only with prior approval.
- Pets must be well-behaved and not left unattended.
- Guests are responsible for any damage or extra cleaning caused by pets.

## 🧹 Cleanliness

- Please keep the space reasonably clean.
- Dispose of trash properly before check-out.

Thank you for your understanding and cooperation, enjoy your stay!
